The show

A conversation, not a spectacle.
A record, not a performance.

No Safe Ground brings Ireland into conversation with the Middle East: places shaped by occupation, partition and resistance; peoples who protected their dignity through it all.

Each episode makes room for journalists, scholars, artists and witnesses to speak at length — beyond the narrow terms of the daily news cycle.
