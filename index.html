Episode 1 — Tawfiq Al-Ghussein

Empire, Exile and the Administration of Palestine

Chelsea, London. October 2020. A photograph: two men, one Palestinian, one Irish, side by side in the foyer of Nell Gwynn House. The caption reads: "When Palestine and Ireland meet there's always bonhomie and solidarity." Six years later, we return to the same foyer.

In this first episode of No Safe Ground, Pól Ó Géibheannaigh sits with writer and witness Tawfiq Al-Ghussein for a long-form conversation on exile, memory, language, and what has happened to the world — and to Palestine — in the years between those two dates.

This is not commentary. It is testimony.
It is not analysis. It is presence.
It is the conversation as documentary, unmediated and uncut.

Recorded at Nell Gwynn House, Chelsea — a building whose Art Deco interiors were completed in the years of the British Mandate, when the ground beneath Palestine was already being redrawn.

Tawfiq Al-Ghussein is the author of The Administration of Palestine: Authority Unresolved (Britain Palestine Project, 2026). His grandfather served on the Arab Higher Committee and was exiled to the Seychelles after the Arab Revolt; his father served on the PLO Executive Committee and chaired the Palestine National Fund from 1984 to 1996.
