EMPIRE, EXILE & THE ADMINISTRATION OF PALESTINE · 58 MIN

Chelsea, London. On 20 October 2020, Pól Ó Geibheannaigh took a photograph of two men, one Palestinian and one Irish, standing together in the foyer of Nell Gwynn House. We return to where we were: six years later, in 2026, he went back to the same place and took the same photograph again — before sitting with writer and witness Tawfiq Al-Ghussein for an unmediated conversation on exile, memory, language and what has happened to Palestine in the years between those two dates.

Get in touch

Recorded at Nell Gwynn House, Chelsea. This is testimony rather than commentary: presence rather than analysis, held in the room and left intact.

Tawfiq Al-Ghussein is the author of The Administration of Palestine: Authority Unresolved.

THE PODCAST

“No safe ground —
until there’s a safe ground.”

No Safe Ground is a long-form interview series about occupation, partition and resistance — and the dignity people keep anyway.

Hosted by journalist, filmmaker and artist Pól Ó Geibheannaigh, each episode brings together the voices building the record while the world looks away.
