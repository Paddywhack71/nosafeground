# No Safe Ground — Style Bible v1.0

## Purpose
A single standard for the No Safe Ground website, episodes, Vimeo, YouTube, podcast listings and social materials. The work must feel patient, serious, intimate and precise.

## Editorial premise
No Safe Ground is a long-form conversation series hosted by Pól Ó Géibheannaigh. It makes space for writers, witnesses and cultural figures discussing rupture, exile, occupation, erasure, return and memory. The conversation, room and silence are part of the work.

## Voice
- Write with calm authority: plain, specific and humane.
- Use British/Irish English.
- Name people, locations, credits and historical context accurately.
- Let the subject carry the drama.
- Prefer “conversation”, “encounter”, “recorded in” and “watch the full episode”.
- Do not use clickbait, “content”, generic podcast hype, emojis in editorial copy, or unsupported claims.

## Visual system
| Role | Colour | HEX |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | Blackened plum | #1B121B |
| Depth | Near-black | #0D0B0D |
| Text | Bone / warm paper | #E9E1D4 |
| Accent | Antique gold | #B8924A |
| Secondary text | Smoke grey | #A79FA1 |

Use an editorial serif for titles and a clear sans-serif for metadata. Maintain generous spacing, thin gold rules and one strong focus per section. Avoid rounded-card interfaces, neon gradients, generic waveform graphics and heavy effects.

## Image and edit direction
Choose portraits, hands, rooms, books, windows, thresholds and place. Preserve shadow, grain, practical light and real texture. Cut for clarity rather than speed. Do not fill every pause with b-roll or music. Use accurate captions.

## Vimeo and website
- Use the official responsive Vimeo player, 16:9, without forced autoplay.
- Keep the player uncluttered and wide.
- Confirm Vimeo embedding is allowed for nosafeground.co.uk before public release.
- Test playback while logged out as well as on desktop and iPhone.

## Episode title format
No Safe Ground — Episode [number]: [Guest Name]

## Release gate
Do not publish until the final edit, approved title/guest spelling, artwork, captions, credits and all public links have been checked on desktop and phone. When in doubt, choose the quieter, more precise option.
